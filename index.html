The Good Jobs Index

This is an annually updating tool that explores which jobs pay enough to live on, and which of those good-paying jobs you can likely get without a four-year Bachelor's degree.

Current Scope: the United States and California, 1976 to today, with plans for additional states and regions to come.

Good job
An occupation that pays a full-time worker at least the median household income (in the US or California, for that year). To mirror the high cost of living, we set the bar high on purpose; see more of our rationale on our Methodology page.
College-optional
An occupation where 49% or less of workers hold a bachelor's degree. In other words, there's a real chance to enter this job without a four-year degree, though many still call for a certificate or community-college credential.
